# Hook Discovery (/docs/community/learning/hook-discovery)

Community-built catalogs and rankings of deployed Uniswap v4 hooks.

Catalogs of deployed Uniswap v4 hooks across networks. Useful for research, due diligence, and finding prior art before designing your own hook.

> [!NOTE]
> These directories index third-party hooks. Always verify hook contracts independently before interacting with them in production.

| Resource                             | Covers                                                             | Maintainer |
| :----------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------- |
| [HookRank](https://hookrank.io/)     | Directory and ranking of deployed Uniswap v4 hooks across networks | Community  |
| [Hook Atlas](https://hookatlas.com/) | Catalog of hook patterns and reference implementations             | Community  |

## Want to add a resource?
If you maintain a hook directory or catalog, we'd love to feature it.

Submit a request through our [help center](https://support.uniswap.org) with the resource name, link, a short description, and your contact info.
