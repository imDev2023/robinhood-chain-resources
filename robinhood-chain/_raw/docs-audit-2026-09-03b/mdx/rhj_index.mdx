# Robinhood RHJ

Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited ("RHJ") is the issuer of Robinhood Stock Tokens. This site provides the issuer information, disclosures, and ongoing notices required in connection with the Stock Tokens.

## About the Issuer

- **Issuer name:** Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited
- **Place of incorporation:** The Issuer is a private limited company incorporated in Jersey.
- **Registered address:** First Floor, La Chasse Chambers, Ten La Chasse, St. Helier, JE2 4UE, Jersey.
- **Email:** [contact.rhj@robinhood.com](mailto:contact.rhj@robinhood.com)
- **Issuer LEI:** 984500ADFHQZ9D6B9A29
- **Registration number:** The Issuer is registered under the registration number 162428.

### Regulatory status

The Issuer is not regulated. However, in connection with the issuance of Stock Tokens, the Issuer has obtained certain consents in Jersey. Such consents do not constitute prudential supervision of the Issuer or an endorsement of its products. The relevant Jersey authorities do not assume any responsibility for the financial soundness of the Issuer or for the accuracy of any statements made, or opinions expressed, in relation to the Issuer or its products.
