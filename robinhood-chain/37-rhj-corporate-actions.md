# Corporate Actions

This page lists corporate actions affecting Robinhood Stock Tokens. Corporate actions can include events such as stock splits, dividends, mergers, name changes, and delistings of an underlying asset.

For each corporate action, Robinhood will publish the relevant adjustment event, details of the corporate action, and information on any adjustments made to the terms and conditions of the affected Stock Tokens.

## Trading during corporate actions

Trading in an affected Stock Token is typically paused while a corporate action is processed. In most cases, placing new orders is unavailable from the early morning on the effective date (around 2 AM CET/CEST) and resumes once processing is complete, typically by the start of the US market day (around 3:30 PM CET/CEST).

During certain corporate actions, open orders for the affected token may be canceled. In limited cases, such as a delisting or liquidation, only sell or redemption activity may be permitted while the action is processed.

## How this relates to the on-chain multiplier

*Original writing. Everything above this line is a capture.*

This section compares the page against `07-building-with-stock-tokens.md` (sections "Multiplier Conversion", "Multiplier Updates" and "ERC-8056 events") and against `31-onchain-verification.md` (sections "Live state at capture", "Multipliers at block 52,437,900" and "Multipliers at block 53,117,114").

**What it adds.**

`07` states that the multiplier is updated "when a corporate action is applied (e.g. a reinvested dividend or a stock split)" but names no schedule and no source of truth for which action caused which update.
This page is that source: it gives the action type, the affected token symbol and contract address, the effective date, a status, and for a cash dividend the gross per-share rate.
It is the only Robinhood-published surface found so far that says *why* a given `UIMultiplierUpdated` was emitted.

It also adds a fact neither `07` nor `31` records: **trading in an affected token is paused while the action is processed**, from about 2 AM CET/CEST on the effective date until about 3:30 PM CET/CEST, and open orders may be cancelled.
`31` observed `oraclePaused() == false` on every token at all three capture blocks, so the archive has never caught a pause in progress; this page explains what that flag is for and gives the roughly 13.5 hour window in which to look for one.

**Where it corroborates the archive.**

The effective dates line up with the `effectiveAt()` timestamps in `31` for four of the tokens whose multiplier moved, in each case landing on the next business day after the listed effective date:

| Token | Feed effective date | `effectiveAt()` in `31` | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ASML | 2026-08-05 (Wed) | 2026-08-06 15:10 UTC | next day |
| COST | 2026-08-07 (Fri) | 2026-08-10 15:10 UTC | next business day |
| AAPL | 2026-08-13 (Thu) | 2026-08-14 15:12 UTC | next day |
| CCL | 2026-08-28 (Fri) | 2026-08-31 15:10 UTC | next business day |
| F | 2026-09-01 (Tue) | 2026-09-02 15:10 UTC | next day |

That confirms the reading in `31` that the 15:10 UTC cluster is a scheduled daily accrual job, and it gives the job a cause.
Note that F's row still reads `In Progress` in the feed even though the on-chain multiplier for F already moved on 2026-09-02; status in this feed is not a reliable indicator that the chain has been written.

**Where it contradicts, or at least fails to reconcile with, the archive.**

Twelve actions in the feed are marked `Completed`: CCL, HWM, CTSH, FIX, SIMO, AAPL, COST, SGOV, UMC, SHY, ASML, BND.
Only five of those twelve tokens (CCL, AAPL, COST, SGOV, ASML) carry a `uiMultiplier()` other than 1.0 at block 53,117,114 per `31`.
**HWM, CTSH, FIX, SIMO, UMC, SHY and BND had a completed cash dividend and are still at exactly 1.0.** This was checked independently on 2026-09-03 against `GET https://api.robinhood.com/rhj/assets`, which `31` establishes is a faithful mirror of chain state: all seven return `currentMultiplier: "1.000000000000000000"` (`_raw/rhj-docs-2026-09-03/api-assets.json`).
Either the multiplier for those tokens is not the mechanism by which the dividend reaches holders, or the write has not happened, or `Completed` on this page means something narrower than "the token has been adjusted".
The docs do not say which, and this archive cannot tell them apart without watching one of those tokens through an event.

The same check kills a plausible-looking shortcut.
All 32 actions marked `In Progress` have `pendingMultiplier: ""` on `/rhj/assets`, including UPS and BND with effective date 2026-09-03, the day of capture.
**An in-progress corporate action does not populate `pendingMultiplier`, and by extension does not schedule `newUIMultiplier()`/`effectiveAt()` ahead of time.** The pre-announcement path documented in `07` under "Multiplier Updates" is real in the interface but is not how these dividends actually arrive: the multiplier simply changes.
That is consistent with `31`'s repeated finding of `newUIMultiplier() == uiMultiplier()` on every token at every capture block, and it means this page, not the chain, is the only forward-looking notice a holder gets.

Conversely, the feed is a rolling window: its earliest entry is 2026-08-05.
It therefore does **not** contain CRWD's 4:1 forward split (multiplier 4.0, `effectiveAt` 2026-07-02), nor the ORCL (2026-07-27), MU (2026-07-24) or DELL (2026-08-03) accruals.
Four of the ten non-unity multipliers in `31` have no explanation in this feed at all.
This matters for the checklist line in `ROBINHOOD-CHAIN.md` section 15 that says to "reconcile against `/rhj/corporate-actions` for the reason behind a change": that only works for changes inside roughly the last month, and the endpoint must be polled and archived if history is wanted.

**What it leaves unexplained, and this is the important gap.**

The page gives a gross cash rate per share and nothing else.
It states no rule for converting that rate into a multiplier delta, and the obvious rule does not hold.
If a dividend of `D` per share were reinvested at a price `P`, the multiplier would scale by `(1 + D/P)`; solving for the implied `P` from the deltas in `31` gives absurd numbers:

| Token | Rate | Multiplier delta | Implied reinvestment price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ASML | $1.817086 | +0.0101323% | ~$17,900 |
| COST | $1.47 | +0.0612040% | ~$2,400 |
| AAPL | $0.27 | +0.0566080% | ~$477 |
| F | $0.15 | +0.0145503% | ~$1,031 |
| SGOV | $0.306812 | +0.211% (Aug 12 to Sep 1) | ~$145 |

None of these is a plausible share price for the ticker, and the error is not a constant factor, so it is not a flat withholding rate either.
**The relationship between the published cash rate and the on-chain multiplier movement is undocumented and, on this evidence, not derivable.** Anyone pricing a Stock Token must read `uiMultiplier()` from the chain and must not attempt to anticipate it from this feed.
That is a stronger warning than `07` currently gives.

I did not verify the underlying share prices independently, so the implied-price column is arithmetic on the two published numbers only, not a claim about market prices.
