# Newsletter & Blog

> Subscribe to the bi-monthly Blockscout newsletter and blog for explorer updates, new chain integrations, product releases, and ecosystem news.

The Blockscout Newsletter is sent out bi-monthly. It includes information on updates, new chains, integrations and other related information. If you use the My Account feature on Blockscout, an email newsletter subscription is automatically enabled when you sign up. Unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of the email.

<Icon icon="flag" iconType="solid" /> You can also [subscribe to receive the newsletter](https://www.blog.blockscout.com/).

<Icon icon="flag" iconType="solid" /> Past newsletters are available on the [Blockscout Blog](https://www.blog.blockscout.com/).
