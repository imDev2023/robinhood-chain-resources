Reading: on V1 the locker's protocolFeeShare of 100 does NOT mean the protocol takes
everything. It routes everything to the FeeCollector, which has protocolShare()=0 and
tokenTreasuryShare()=0 and therefore burns the launched-token side and pays the entire
WETH side to the creator. Net effect: creator receives 100% of the ETH-denominated fees
and the token side is permanently removed from supply. This matches the 2026-07-14
announcement that trading fees were set to 100% for creators.
