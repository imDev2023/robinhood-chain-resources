Graduation on the live V2 launchpad is therefore a fully diluted market cap of
20 WETH, about $48,000 at the $2,399.22 ETH price the same API reported.
A fresh launch opens at about 1.3557 WETH FDV, roughly $3,253, which is why every
new coin shows about 6.78% progress the moment it is created.
Graduation has no contract effect: the position never moves and nothing migrates.

Pool WETH balances were also read and do NOT track graduationPct, confirming the
metric is a price/market-cap measure rather than a liquidity-raised measure.
