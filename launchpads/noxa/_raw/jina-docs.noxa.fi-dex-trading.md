Title: Trading on NOXA DEX

URL Source: https://docs.noxa.fi/dex/trading/

Markdown Content:
Trading on NOXA DEX works exactly like Uniswap.

1.   Go to [dex.noxa.fi](https://dex.noxa.fi/)
2.   Connect your wallet
3.   Select the chain you want to trade on
4.   Start swapping

## Discovering Tokens & Pools

[Section titled “Discovering Tokens & Pools”](https://docs.noxa.fi/dex/trading/#discovering-tokens--pools)

Use the [Explore page](https://dex.noxa.fi/#/explore) to discover tokens and pools on NOXA DEX.

Tokens launched through NOXA Fun are marked with a **“via NOXA Fun”** badge, so you can easily identify tokens that were created on our launchpad with locked liquidity.

1.   Select the token you want to sell (top input)
2.   Select the token you want to buy (bottom input)
3.   Enter the amount
4.   Review the quote
5.   Click “Swap”
6.   Confirm the transaction in your wallet

### Slippage Settings

[Section titled “Slippage Settings”](https://docs.noxa.fi/dex/trading/#slippage-settings)

For volatile tokens (like newly launched memecoins), you may need to adjust slippage:

1.   Click the settings gear icon
2.   Set slippage tolerance (e.g., 5%, 10%, 20%)
3.   Higher slippage = more likely to succeed, but worse price

**Tip:** New tokens on NOXA Fun use the 1% fee tier V3 pool, which helps with price impact.

## Trading NOXA Fun Tokens

[Section titled “Trading NOXA Fun Tokens”](https://docs.noxa.fi/dex/trading/#trading-noxa-fun-tokens)

Tokens launched on NOXA Fun targeting NOXA DEX are immediately tradeable:

1.   Find the token address (from NOXA Fun or shared by the creator)
2.   Paste it into the swap interface
3.   Trade as normal

The V3 pool exists from the moment the token is created.

When trading a new token:

1.   The DEX will ask you to confirm the import
2.   Verify the contract address carefully
3.   Acknowledge the risks of trading new tokens

**Always verify contract addresses** - scammers may create tokens with similar names.

*   **Swap fee:** Depends on the pool (0.05%, 0.3%, or 1% for V3)
*   **Gas:** Standard EVM transaction gas

## Tips for Degen Trading

[Section titled “Tips for Degen Trading”](https://docs.noxa.fi/dex/trading/#tips-for-degen-trading)

1.   **Check liquidity** before large trades - new tokens may have limited depth
2.   **Use reasonable slippage** - too low and your tx fails, too high and you get front-run
3.   **Verify contracts** - always double-check token addresses
4.   **DYOR** - new tokens are risky, only trade what you can afford to lose
