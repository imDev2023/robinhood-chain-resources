NOXA is the first DegenFi protocol, built for trenchers who trade memecoins and aren’t afraid to gamble hard on new tokens.

We deploy a complete DeFi stack on new blockchains **before anyone else**. When a new hyped chain launches and there’s no DEX available yet, NOXA is already there.

### NOXA Fun (Launchpad)

[Section titled “NOXA Fun (Launchpad)”](https://docs.noxa.fi/introduction/#noxa-fun-launchpad)

Our main product. A hybrid token launchpad at [fun.noxa.fi](https://fun.noxa.fi/) that combines instant V3 DEX trading with graduation mechanics:

*   **Single-sided V3 LP** - Tokens are immediately tradeable on Uniswap V3 upon creation
*   **Locked liquidity forever** - LP is locked in our locker, never moves
*   **Graduation mechanics** - Same gamification as bonding curves, but no LP migration
*   **No token taxes** - Uses built-in V3 fee tiers instead of custom bonding curve fees
*   **Creator fees** - Token creators earn and can claim fees from trading activity
*   **Multi-DEX support** - Launch on NOXA DEX, Uniswap, PancakeSwap, or other supported DEXes

A Uniswap V2 + V3 fork at [dex.noxa.fi](https://dex.noxa.fi/) with no contract modifications. It exists as the fallback DEX on chains where nothing else is available yet.

Traditional bonding curve launchpads have problems:

*   LP migrates when the token “graduates” - creates risk and friction
*   Custom token taxes built into the bonding curve
*   Tokens aren’t on a real DEX until after graduation

NOXA Fun solves this with a hybrid approach:

1.   Tokens trade on V3 DEX immediately - no waiting, no migration
2.   LP is locked forever in our locker
3.   Graduation is a milestone marker with on-chain events and UI celebrations
4.   No token taxes - just standard V3 fee tiers
5.   Creators earn fees from trading activity

Best of both worlds: instant DEX trading AND graduation mechanics.

Our community is comprised of trenchers - people who live in the memecoin trenches. No VCs, no pretense, just degens building for degens.
