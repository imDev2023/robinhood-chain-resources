## NOXA Lite

A minimal, self-contained interface to explore tokens that were launched via NOXA launchpad contracts.

*   Wallet-native: connect a wallet to browse.
*   Static and non-custodial: blockchain data is read through an RPC endpoint you choose.
*   Tokens listed here were created by users on a permissionless protocol. Nothing is endorsed or vetted. Trade nothing you cannot afford to lose.
