## Contract Addresses

[Section titled “Contract Addresses”](https://docs.noxa.fi/launchpad/chains/#contract-addresses)

See the [NOXA Fun Contracts](https://docs.noxa.fi/contracts/noxa-fun/) page for all contract addresses per chain.

Want your DEX supported on NOXA Fun? See our [DEX Integration](https://docs.noxa.fi/integrations/dex/) guide.

Requirements:

*   Uniswap V3 or PancakeSwap V3 fork (unmodified contracts)
*   Deployed on a chain we support
