## export private key (App)

How to export your Bagsapp wallet to Phantom

1. Open Bagsapp.

2. Tap the profile icon in the bottom menu (first on the right).

3. Tap the settings icon in the top-left corner.

4. Select “Export Private Key”, then “Reveal Private Key” for the wallet you want to export. (Do this in a safe environment.)

5. Tap “EXPORT WALLET” → then “Copy Key”. Your private key will be copied to your clipboard. ◦ ⚠️ Important: Save your private key somewhere safe (e.g., write it down on paper) for future use.

## export private key (Web)

How to export your Bagsapp wallet to Phantom

1. Visit bags.fm.

2. Login with your credentials.

3. Tap your username/pfp in the top-right corner.

4. Select “My Wallets”, then “Export Private Key” for the wallet you want to export. (Do this in a safe environment.)

5. Tap “Copy Key”. Your private key will be copied to your clipboard. ◦ ⚠️ Important: Save your private key somewhere safe (e.g., write it down on paper) for future use.
