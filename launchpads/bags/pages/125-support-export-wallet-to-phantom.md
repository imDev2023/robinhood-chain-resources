Title: Export Wallet to Phantom

URL Source: https://support.bags.fm/en/articles/13434595-export-wallet-to-phantom

Markdown Content:
1. Open Bagsapp.

2. Tap the profile icon in the bottom menu (first on the right).

3. Tap the settings icon in the top-left corner.

4. Select "My Wallets", “Export Private Key”, then “Reveal Private Key” for the wallet you want to export. (Do this in a safe environment.) 5. Tap “EXPORT WALLET” → then “Copy Key”. Your private key will be copied to your clipboard. ◦ ⚠️ Important: Save your private key somewhere safe (e.g., write it down on paper) for future use.

6. Open Phantom.

7. Tap the account icon in the top-left corner.

8. Select “Add/Connect Wallet” → “Import Private Key.”

9. Paste your key into the Private Key field.

10. (Optional) Give your wallet a name.

11. Tap “Import.” Your Bags wallet should now be available in Phantom.
