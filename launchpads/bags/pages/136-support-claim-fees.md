How to claim fees

Written by bags support

January 20, 2026

Here's how to claim fees:

1.   Go to [https://bags.fm/](https://bags.fm/) 
2.   Click 'Verify with X' to sign in with X account

3.   Go to your profile

4.   If you have rewards to claim, there will be a green 'Claim' button next to the respective token
