Why can't I change royalty/fee recipient/receiver?

Written by bags support

January 20, 2026

We cannot allow changes to royalty recipients, as doing so would undermine trust and integrity within our community. Imagine a token that gained strong support because its fees went to a charity or a respected figure. If the creator could later redirect those royalties to themselves, it would feel deceptive, harm our reputation, and discourage people from using our launchpad for this feature. That’s why this policy is in place.
