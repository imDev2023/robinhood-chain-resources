How is liquidity provided?

Written by bags support

January 20, 2026

Liquidity comes from different places depending on the stage of the token:

​

Pre-bond: DBC - Liquidity is virtual. Only one side (the token) is needed, and the bonding curve simulates liquidity until a threshold of 85 SOL is reached.

Post-bond: DAMM v2 - Liquidity comes from users who provide it by depositing tokens into the pool. These deposits make up the actual liquidity that supports trading.

​

Feel free to check the Meteora documentation for detailed information!
