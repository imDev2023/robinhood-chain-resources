How do I transfer my crypto to fiat?

Written by bags support

January 20, 2026

If you’d like to convert your crypto into real fiat (e.g. EUR, USD), you’ll need to use a centralized exchange (CEX) such as Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, etc.​

These platforms allow you to link a bank account and withdraw funds.​

⚠️ Please always make sure you copy the correct wallet address from your CEX, as blockchain transactions can’t be reversed. For any issues with bank withdrawals, it’s best to reach out directly to your exchange’s support team.
