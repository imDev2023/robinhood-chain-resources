I received an error trying to launch a token. What is going on?

Written by bags support

January 20, 2026

Sorry to hear you’re having trouble creating your token. Errors like 400, 404, 500 status codes, or a failure to launch your token can happen for a few common reasons:

*   404 = make sure the user you are sharing fees with actually exists on Twitter

*   500 = make sure you have at least 0.1 SOL, your Token name is not over 32 characters and your ticker not over 10 characters

*   Solana network congestion or downtime

*   Invalid or incomplete input (e.g. missing token name, symbol, or image)

*   Wallet connection issues or insufficient Sol for fees

*   Temporary backend hiccups

### Quick Fixes:

*   Try refreshing and reconnecting your wallet

*   Double check that all fields are filled out correctly

*   Make sure your wallet has enough SOL (usually at least 0.1 SOL for fees)

*   If the issue persists, try again in a few minutes
