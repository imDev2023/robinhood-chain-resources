January 20, 2026

1.   Upload an image via top circular icon

2.   Input token name—not over 32 characters

3.   Input token ticker—not over 10 characters

4.   Optional—input X account @ of who you'd like to share fees with

5.   We recommend holding at least 0.2 SOL on your wallet to ensure network fees are covered
