How much sol do I need to launch a token on your platform?

Written by bags support

January 20, 2026

To launch a token on Bags, you’ll need approximately 0.2 SOL to cover network fees and transaction costs. We recommend keeping a little extra in your wallet just in case the Solana network is busy.
