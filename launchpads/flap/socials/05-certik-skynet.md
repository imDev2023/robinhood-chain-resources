CertiK Skynet carries Flap as a Launchpad and Smart Contract Platform project.

Facts taken from the page:

- Ecosystems listed: **BSC and Base only**. Robinhood Chain is not tracked by Skynet, so no Skynet score covers the deployment this archive is about.
- Socials linked: CoinGecko `flap`, LinkedIn `flap-sh`, X 100K, Telegram 18K, Discord.
- Data sourced by RootData, `https://www.rootdata.com/projects/detail/Flap?k=MTMxOTg=`.
- One-line description: "Flap is a social fair launch platform for tokenizing interactions and trading memecoins on EVM chains."
- Token & Contracts: Not Available. Listed Date: Not Available.

The docs say the V2 and V4 protocols and Flap Tax Token V1 were audited by CertiK, and that Protocol V5 with Tax Token V2 and PreLaunch V1 was audited by BlockSec.
Both BlockSec reports are archived at `_raw/docs/files/blocksec_flap_protocol_v5_v1.0-signed.pdf` and `_raw/docs/files/blocksec_flap_tax_token_v1.0-signed.pdf`.
No published audit covers **Tax Token V3**, which is one of the two token types Robinhood Chain accepts, nor Portal v5.21.2, which is the version live on chain 4663.
