# Someone may owe you fees.

A Fee Share launch sends its creator fees to an X account rather than to a vault — no wallet needed, and no say in the matter. If that account is yours, check what has built up and send it somewhere you control.

How this works

- 1

A creator names your account

On a Fee Share launch there is no vault. The launch points its creator fees at a wallet derived from an X handle, and the pons escrow credits that address from the first trade.

- 2

You sign in with X

That is the whole proof. Your handle is read from the sign-in rather than typed in, so nobody can start a claim for an account they do not hold, and there is nothing to post.

- 3

We claim and forward

The escrow pays whoever calls it, so the derived wallet has to make the claim itself. It does, then forwards everything to your address in the same run.

Fees reach the escrow only after the token trades, so a launch from an hour ago can still read empty. Nothing expires — whatever is credited stays there until claimed.
