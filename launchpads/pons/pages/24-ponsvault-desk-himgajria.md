## Where the 5% goes

Every buy and every sell pays 5%. It is taken automatically — there is nothing to claim from a trade, and nothing you need to do to make it happen.

- 2%

### Paid to stakers in CPER

Converted into CPER and shared out between everyone who has staked, in proportion to how much they staked. You claim it whenever you like.

- 2%

### Buys COPPERINU back and burns it

Spent buying the token off the market, then sent to a dead address. Those tokens are gone for good, so the supply only ever shrinks.

- 1%

### Goes to pons

The protocol fee charged on every pons launch. It is the same for every token on the platform.

Staked tokens are never burned, and staking never locks them up — you can withdraw at any time. The payout runs automatically once enough fees have built up to be worth the gas.
