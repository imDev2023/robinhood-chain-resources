# pons

> Launch fixed-supply tokens on Robinhood Chain.

pons is the Robinhood Chain launchpad for fixed-supply tokens. Explore newest launches, create a token, and trade from the pool.

## Product
- Explore newest fixed-supply token launches on Robinhood Chain
- Create a token from the launchpad form
- Trade launched tokens from the pool page
