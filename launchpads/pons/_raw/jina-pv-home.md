How it works

## Three steps. One signature.

1.   01
### Pick a pair & vault

Choose an approved quote asset and a vault — Staking, Stake & Burn, or RWA Dividend.

2.   02
### Launch in one tx

We deploy the token through pons v2, create the vault, and route fees to it.

3.   03
### It runs itself

Trading accrues fees. Anyone can press run — or our keeper does it for you.

### Staking

Live

Holders stake your token and earn creator fees in the pairing asset, pro rata.

### Stake & Burn

Live

Stake to earn. Half the fees buy the token back and burn it.

### RWA Dividend

Live

Fees buy a tokenized stock. Holders claim by balance — no staking.

### Lottery

Soon

Fees fill a pot. Holders enter. A commit–reveal draw pays one wallet.
