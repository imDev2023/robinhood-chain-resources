Say hello to Pools, a new launchpad built for Robinhood Chain. Launch a token, discover new ones, and trade them end to end at [pools.trade](https://pools.trade/?utm_medium=blog&utm_source=blog&utm_campaign=pools-202608&utm_content=pools).

### [](https://blog.uniswap.org/pools-trade-a-new-way-to-launch-on-robinhood-chain#built-by-the-most-trusted-team-in-defi)Built by the most trusted team in DeFi

Pools is built by Uniswap Labs and comes with:

*   **Autocompounding liquidity:** LP fees autocompound back into the locked position
*   **Permanent locked liquidity:** Tokens are in a protocol-held pool that cannot be removed by the creator
*   **Optional creator fees:** Creators can turn on an optional fee at launch and receive 0.05% of the 25bps fee
*   **No launchpad fees:** Each pool launches a standard Uniswap pool, with a 0.25% LP fee that autocompounds to grow locked liquidity. This is a fraction of the standard ~1% on other launchpads
*   **Sniping mitigation:** Creators buy in the same block the token is launched, preventing snipers from being the first to buy a new token

### [](https://blog.uniswap.org/pools-trade-a-new-way-to-launch-on-robinhood-chain#how-launches-work)How launches work

There are two ways to launch a token on Pools. Both start with a fixed supply of 1 billion and end in a Uniswap v4 pool. Fees autocompound to grow locked liquidity.

|  | Crowd Launch | Instant Launch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Format** | 4-hour launch window | Live immediately |
| **Mechanism** | TWAP bids mitigate bundling | Classic bonding curve |
| **Graduation** | 10K launch FDV or bids are refunded | No requirement |
| **End state** | Deeper liquidity, locked forever | Liquidity locked forever |

**Crowd Launch: Bundle resistant, fairer distribution**

Your token launches over a four-hour window. Everyone bids a budget, and bids fill gradually over the reminder of the launch window. Price moves with demand, so earlier bids get better prices.

Every budget fills in full by the end. The token is tradable if the launch reaches $10k FDV. If it falls short, all orders are refunded.

**Instant Launch: Fast and simple**

Your token is live the moment you create it. The price climbs as people buy, and you can swap in and out anytime.

Head to [pools.trade](https://pools.trade/?utm_medium=blog&utm_source=blog&utm_campaign=pools-202608&utm_content=pools) to get started.

**To launch:**

1.   Choose Crowd Launch or Instant Launch

2.   Add an image, ticker, and description

3.   Launch

**To trade:**

1.   Browse the feed on [pools.trade](https://pools.trade/?utm_medium=blog&utm_source=blog&utm_campaign=pools-202608&utm_content=pools)

2.   Buy a token or bid on a Crowd Launch

3.   Claim your tokens once a graduated Crowd Launch ends
