## What each one does

**LiquidityLauncher** `0x0000FffFBE8efE702c8703aE3477FF5dE3d319C0`
Entry point for every launch.
`createToken`, `depositToken`, `distributeToken`, `distributeWithNative`, all wrapped in `multicall`. v3.2.0.

**ContinuousClearingAuctionFactory** `0x000000001F26a0044BaA66024e7b6599c61963F8`
CREATE2 factory for Crowd Launch auction contracts. v2.1.0, current.

**ContinuousClearingAuctionFactoryV2_0_0** `0x00cCa200BF124dBfA848937c553864f4B4CE0632`
CCA factory v2.0.0, superseded by the 2026-07-09 redeploy.

**TWAAuctionFactoryV1** `0xcccccccae7503cac057829bf2811de42e16e0bd5`
The app bundle labels this "v1 TWA auction factory"; on chain it verifies as `ContinuousClearingAuctionFactory`.
Superseded.

**TickDataLens** `0xc3C65F5453A3674aDb693cbdA3C842545cD30f53`
`CCALens`, the read helper the frontend uses for auction tick data.

**InstantLaunchStrategy-creator-fees** `0x23f8209572b4a1C2AD88A42749E830791Fb027f1`
Instant Launch with creator fees.
Current.
`LP_FEE 2500`, `TICK_SPACING 25`, `TOTAL_SUPPLY 1e27`, `initialTick 198050`.

**InstantLaunchStrategy-no-creator-fees** `0xAD44D55E7f8337C3cE113fBb591486E85be104b2`
Instant Launch without creator fees.
Current.
Same immutables, `beneficiaryVault` is the zero address.

**FeeSplitter-creator-fees** `0xeFF166AAf189323c58dc27eD1206EB2C37FaACDf`
Holds every creator-fee Instant Launch position permanently.
Splits: 40% native to the vault, 60% native and 100% token to the compounding recipient.

**FeeSplitter-no-creator-fees** `0x222D6d4f1ce59b0d48D5505114eC8Addc90A4359`
Holds every no-creator-fee position permanently.
Splits: 100% native and 100% token to the compounding recipient.

**UERC20BeneficiaryVault** `0xd35E9CA72F64C7F93BE30fad67524323396B36D7`
Holds the creator fee share.
Registration mints a transferable ERC-721; only its holder can claim.

**CompoundingClaimRecipient** `0xf9526Dd3361fe0ba6b7a99533ed471D3E808E99a`
Receives the compounding share and pushes it back into the same locked position as liquidity.

**BuybackAndBurnClaimRecipient** `0xa1ba4CC12654D2b188e3ba77dc86c75cA47f1A4e`
Alternative claim recipient that releases fees to whoever burns 500,000 tokens.
Deployed but not wired into either pools.trade launch model.

**VestingClaimRecipient** `0xeF451B293ED8C61d20f7d13ef336a496F0cc2c26`
Holds a renounced beneficiary NFT and releases fees downstream at a capped rate per block.
Not in the pools.trade launch path.

**UniversalRouterStrategy** `0x1242c9439d589cAE85E121B1f79f2aF51e91DCEE`
Runs a caller-supplied Universal Router route so a launch and the creator buy fit in one transaction.
This is the "Buy at launch" switch and the sniping mitigation.

**LBPStrategy** `0x05d552391067389EE44fec3924157ed33F976000`
Crowd Launch.
Creates the CCA auction, then migrates the raise plus the reserved 50% of supply into a v4 pool. v3.1.1.

**LBPStrategyV3_0_0** `0x095e38a2135aeBcfFa98A5B6911591937f912000`
LBPStrategy v3.0.0, deprecated.

**InitializerHook** `0xD462a559337859369EF271814851A18F496ba000`
Base hook restricting pool initialization to a preset address.
Not used by either standard pools.trade launch (both use a hookless pool).

**TokenSplitter** `0x4F5E3FBb9745358A92Da5674305FAb8D2B8a73cE`
Minimal strategy splitting a distribution across recipients.
Not used by either standard pools.trade launch.

**UERC20Factory** `0x000000e200088D55C39a11F609E5F667729ad49b`
Creates the UERC20 token.
Every pools.trade token is minted here, 1e27 supply, 18 decimals, with name, description, X url and image URI.

**InstantLaunchStrategy-0729-fees** `0x60D73b21cDf2EA846ab3d58699BBbb8F29d72491`
Superseded Instant Launch strategy.
See the directory README for its generation and pairing.

**InstantLaunchStrategy-0729-nofees** `0xFCe92C70f1fc017b72f6DD7a00D9E38725C7fBd1`
Superseded Instant Launch strategy.
See the directory README for its generation and pairing.
