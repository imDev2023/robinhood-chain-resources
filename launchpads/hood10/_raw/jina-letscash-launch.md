# launch a coin

One transaction deploys the coin with a fixed supply, seeds a Uniswap pool on Robinhood Chain, and locks the liquidity forever. You can buy in first, before anyone else sees it.

## how it trades

Set once, at launch. None of it can be changed afterwards, not by you and not by us.

What it trades against

ETH![Image 33](https://letscash.fun/quote/usdg.svg)USDG

The pool trades against ETH, and fees pay out in ETH. What every coin here has always done.

Total supply

1 billion coins

type any size 

1B 10B 100B 1T 1Qa

Or type any size between 1 billion and 1 quadrillion. "420.69t", "69,420,000,000,000" and "1 quadrillion" all work.

The whole supply is minted at launch and locked into the pool, whichever you pick. Ten times the coins means a tenth of the price each and ten times as many in every wallet. The fee, your share of it, and the share of the coin any amount of ETH buys are identical at every size. The opening valuation lands within about 2%, because a pool can only start on one of its fixed price steps.

How the fee is used

standard self burn

The creator's share is paid to your wallet in ETH, claimable any time, for as long as the coin trades.

Who receives the fees

Your wallet Another wallet Split it

The creator's share pays the wallet you launch from in ETH, claimable any time for as long as the coin trades. You can hand the stream to another address later.

What a trade pays

1%3%5%10%

Whichever you pick, the platform keeps 0.3%. Every rung above this one is yours. A higher fee earns more per trade and gives traders more reason to trade something else; it cannot be changed after launch.

0.3% platform buys & burns CASHCAT, funds the treasury

0.7%creator paid to your wallet in ETH

Fees are always collected in ETH, never in your coin, so nobody has to sell it to get paid. The rate is fixed at launch and cannot be changed afterwards, not by you and not by us.

## airdrop

[how airdrops work ↗](https://letscash.fun/docs#airdrop)

Optional. Sets part of your first buy aside to hand out later: to holders, early supporters, whoever you pick. It goes into a vault at launch and leaves along a recipient list you publish on chain, any time in the next 7 days.

An airdrop comes out of your first buy, so it needs one. Set a first buy above and this opens up.
