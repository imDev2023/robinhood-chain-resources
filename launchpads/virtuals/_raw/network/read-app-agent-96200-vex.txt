### How It Works

VEX — the verifiable AI agent for on-chain capital. Most agents ask for your trust; VEX proves it. The model proposes, but the runtime decides what actually executes: every action is enforced by your own rules before signing — preventing hallucinated trades, wrong wallets, or oversized positions — and recorded to a tamper-proof, on-chain-anchored audit log. The result is a track record that's provably complete and impossible to rewrite. Enforce your AI. Prove every trade.

### Roadmap

Roadmap

Next (sequential)

- Desktop app launch — local runtime, custody separation, multi-chain (EVM + Solana), live integrations: Polymarket, Jupiter, KyberSwap, Khalani, DexScreener

- ERC-8004 identity attestation — on-chain agent identity registration with externalized verification results

- Verifiable record layer — rejection rows, per-row hashes, sequence numbers, and thesis hashes, with day-one public timestamping and on-chain Merkle anchoring under completeness commitments

- Verified copy trading — bond vaults and the copy qualification gate open the market; followers copy only proven strategists, each within their own policy limits

### Token Utility

① Agent Identity (ERC-8004) — Agents are registered on-chain via ERC-8004, with verification results posted as attestations. Identity and reputation live on top of $VEX.
② Bond — Becoming a copyable strategist requires staking a per-identity $VEX bond — the sybil-defense layer that sets qualification, boost, and priority.
③ Execution Fee — A transparent, volume-based fee on trades executed through VEX, shown as a line item, never hidden in slippage.
④ Buyback — Execution fees and copy settlement flow to open-market $VEX buybacks from day one. No emissions, no inflation.
⑤ Subscription — Free / Pro / Advanced / Team tiers, from local chat to always-on autonomous runtime.
