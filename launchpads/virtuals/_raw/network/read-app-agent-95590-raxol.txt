Raxol is the FOSS agent runtime that transacts on Virtuals ACP through Xochi.fi where the execution happens. Xochi is essentially a privacy Cowswap available across 5 EVM chains, and now also on Robinhood Chain.

It is the first bridge for private swaps native to Robinhood Chain.

@Axol_io is the team building the Raxol agent and underlying infra.

https://github.com/DROOdotFOO/raxol

### How It Works

1. Sign an intent. You say what you want.

2. Solvers compete. Filled from 6* chains in ~2s (P95 &lt;6 seconds).

3. Settle privately. Stealth by default. Your keys, your crypto.

*We will keep adding chains

### Roadmap

6 Chains Live: ETH, BASE, OP, ARB, POLY, Robinhood Chain L3

SOON: TRON, SOL

Supported Assets: USDC, USDT, USDG (Paxos/RobinHood), WETH/ETH

We will be adding more stables to allow your agents to trade FX through our Agentic Dark Pool.

### Additional Details

All L1 transactions have stealth addresses, we will enable through our [PXE-Bridge](https://github.com/xochi-fi/pxe-bridge) post audit.
This will enable L2-privacy crosschain.

We are accepted into Ethereum Subsidy Program and undergoing auditor selection.
