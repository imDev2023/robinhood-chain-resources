Summary

This proposal seeks DAO approval to align incentives between the community, token holders and core contributors along the journey to $40 per $VIRTUAL. Three distinct milestones have been set: $10, $20 and $40 – validated by a 30-day TWAP on Binance spot and a $10M+ daily trading volume floor. Each milestone being hit will correspond to a grant allocation of 2% of $VIRTUAL supply to Virgen Labs (the team), totalling up to 6% (60M tokens) if all targets are achieved. After hitting the milestone, the grants will be unlocked over 36 months (0.013% of supply per week) via onchain smart contracts. No new tokens are minted; DAO treasury share would decrease from 34% to 28% only if all targets ($40 per $VIRTUAL) are reached.

For avoidance of doubt, “Virgen Labs” does not refer to an actual entity, but collectively comprises of core contributors and team members.

Background

- Team History: The original 15% team allocation was granted in 2021 and fully vested by 2023. These tokens were earned before the protocol’s pivot and growth.

- DAO Treasury: 34% of $VIRTUAL supply remains under DAO control. This proposal reallocates a portion to accelerate future growth based on performance without introducing inflation.

- Market Conditions: With $VIRTUAL trading around $1.50, the first milestone implies a ~6.7× price appreciation, aligning rewards strictly with token holder upside.

Benefits to Token Holders

- Extreme Alignment – Virgen Labs is incentivized to deliver 7×–27× price gains through sustainable business practices, legitimate growth, and value-driven initiatives for token holders before any tokens are distributed. No milestone, no tokens.
(Example: $1.50 → $10 adds $8.5 B in market cap before 20 M token unlock happens over 3 years.)

- No Dilution Before Value – Circulating supply remains unchanged until milestones hit. Weekly unlock post-transfer stays negligible (0.013% of supply).

- Team Growth & M&A – We have 50 FTE today in the core contributor team. Achieving our vision of being the largest onchain AI ecosystem requires us to relentlessly hire the best talent globally and even acquire external teams.

- Clear Market Signal – Milestone-based progress provides a clear signal of ambition and intent to the market, fueling momentum and organic attention.

- Onchain Streaming: Upon the milestone being achieved, the Foundation (via the DAO multisig) will transfer the 2M $VIRTUAL to an onchain token distribution smart contract (e.g. Sablier) that will linearly release the tokens over 36 months (0.013% of supply per week) without need for further approval.

Conclusion

The above proposes a zero-inflation, milestone-triggered grant that rewards Virgen Labs only after delivering massive upside for token holders. It converts dormant treasury assets into a high-conviction, high-performance incentive without giving anything away upfront.
