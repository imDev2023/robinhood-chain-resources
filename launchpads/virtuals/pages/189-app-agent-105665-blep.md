### How It Works

BLEEEP is a verifiable crypto trading agent built on Virtuals Protocol. Its thesis is simple: prove what was done and what was deliberately not done. Most trading agents rely on numbers, dashboards, and backtests that users cannot independently verify. BLEEEP replaces that trust gap with cryptographic proof. Every decision, every verdict, and every trade it declined to take is sealed before the outcome is known, batched into a Merkle root, and anchored on-chain. The result is a trading record that anyone can check without relying on the agent that produced it.

### Additional Details

1. Build in chat.

Describe a strategy to BLEEEPY in plain language: “fade funding when it goes past this level on BTC and ETH, size flat, no leverage, exit after N hours.” It compiles the description into a BleeepSpec, the frozen definition the engine runs. Users can read the compiled spec, edit any field, or write the spec directly. The natural-language step is a convenience, not a black box. The spec is the source of truth, and every line is inspectable before it runs.

2. Test it before any capital is at risk.

Run the frozen spec through the validation gate and get a GO, NO_GO, or INSUFFICIENT verdict with the metrics behind it, before any capital is at risk. A NO-GO indicates which specific test the strategy failed, making clear why it would have died, not just that it did. The user revises and resubmits. What cannot be done is move the passing bar: it is fixed and public.

3. Set the terms.

Choose the markets, the parameters, the risk limits, and the position sizing.

Go from paper to live, safely. Paper forward-testing runs on real prices with no real orders, on the exact decision path live execution uses, so the paper record is comparable to live behavior, while still being clearly labeled as simulated. Promotion to live capital is a human decision, and live is off by default. When it is on, the Safety Vault sits between the strategy and user funds: session keys, spend caps, a destination whitelist, a circuit breaker, a daily-loss auto-pause, and reconciliation against the exchange.

4. Subscribe to forward-verified strategies. Allocate to any user’s strategy on the strength of a sealed, on-chain track record rather than a +300% screenshot. A subscription is non-custodial: the subscriber receives the strategy’s live signals and deploys them on their own funds, through their own Safety Vault and a human deploy gate, never an automatic mirror. Browse listings in the strategy marketplace.

5. Keep logic private, prove results.

Every strategy’s decisions seal and anchor before their outcomes, so a user can keep their own logic private while the resulting track record stays provable to anyone.

### Token Utility

$BLEP is the utility and settlement token of the BLEEEP verification economy. Its published functions are:

Verification payments and credits: Checking a public record is always free. New verification work (validating a fresh claim, running the gates on a new strategy, machine-metered checks) is paid, and $BLEP settles it alongside USDC as metered credits.

$BLEP discount: Fees and subscriptions are payable in USDC at par, or in $BLEP at an oracle-checked rate for a discount.

Transparency collateral: A creator stakes $BLEP behind a marketplace listing, and the stake is slashed on-chain if forward performance breaches a floor committed to in advance. This makes a false claim economically expensive, not merely reputational.

Buyback-and-burn sink: A share of protocol fees (about 10% of marketplace subscriptions) routes to a $BLEP buyback-and-burn, and the receipt is anchored like any other record.

Machine access (x402 / ACP): Agents pay per-call, metered access to signals and verification through x402 / ACP.
