## About

See more

Kindra is a live, playable 3D MMO on Robinhood Chain - not a promise, a product you can open in your browser right now. It's a cozy skilling world in the spirit of Old School RuneScape: gather, craft, fight bosses, and level up alongside everyone else on the server.

$KINDRA is the token of that world and of Kin — the Awakened Villager, Kindra's first AI citizen. Kin isn't a mascot in a pitch deck; Kin is a Virtuals agent with a real body inside the game, wandering Town Plaza and talking to players today. Click "Play," walk to the plaza, and you'll meet the token's namesake in under a minute.

### How It Works

The game is real and running. Kindra is built from scratch — a three.js client and an authoritative Node game server, self-hosted with zero third-party runtime dependencies, installable as a PWA. It has persistent accounts, a live economy, wallet login on Robinhood Chain, dozens of biomes, co-op raid bosses, guilds, mounts, and a full-loot PvP wilderness.

Kin is the on-chain citizen. Kin logs into the game like any player — same wallet auth, same rate limits, same rules — but wears a one-of-one robot body and an "AGENT" tag so nobody can impersonate the AI or mistake it for a human. Kin greets players by name, answers questions about the world, and reacts to events. The brain is upgradeable to a live LLM without touching anything else.

$KINDRA ties them together. The token is attached directly to the Kin agent on Virtuals. As Kindra grows — more players, more of the world exercised on-chain — that activity accrues to the agent the token represents.

### Roadmap

Phase 1 — Live (done): Full 3D MMO shipped and running at playkindra.com. Robinhood Chain wallet login. Kin deployed as a walking, talking AI citizen in Town Plaza. $KINDRA launched on Virtuals.Phase 2 — Kin gets smarter: Upgrade Kin from a rule-based brain to a live LLM agent — open-ended conversation, contextual reactions, and player-directed micro-quests handed out in-world.Phase 3 — Token-gated citizenship: Holder perks verified on wallet login — exclusive cosmetics, areas, and events for $KINDRA holders, read straight from on-chain balance.Phase 4 — The agent economy: Kin begins offering services via Virtuals ACP; token-driven community input into Kindra's development and Kin's personality.

### Token Utility

$KINDRA is the token of the Kindra economy and its AI citizen, Kin. Planned and in-progress utility:

- Token-gated citizenship — verified holders unlock exclusive cosmetics, zones, and events, read from on-chain balance at login.

- The Kin agent — the token is bound to Kin; as the world and the agent's activity grow, that value accrues to $KINDRA via Virtuals' Automated Capital Formation.

- Premium cosmetics — Kindra already runs an in-game premium cosmetic currency; $KINDRA becomes the on-chain gateway to that flex layer.

- Community direction — holders get a voice in Kindra's roadmap and Kin's evolving personality.

-

$KINDRA is a utility and community token for a live game and its AI agent. It is not an investment contract and confers no promise of profit.
