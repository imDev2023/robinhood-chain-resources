## About

See more

Mintly in partnership with Virtuals and Origin Protocol is an agentic, self-custody yield protocol that makes earning on chain returns as simple as making a deposit. Users deposit ETH or USDC once, and Mintly's AI agent handles everything else: monitoring rates, selecting strategies, and rebalancing automatically to capture the best available risk-adjusted yield. No dashboards, no protocol-hopping, no DeFi expertise required. Funds remain in the user's own smart contract at all times. Mintly is built on Ethereum and powered by Origin Protocol's audited yield strategies, launching its MLY token through the Virtuals launchpad on Robinhood Chain.

### How It Works

Every Mintly user gets a personal, user-owned strategy contract. Deposits never enter a shared pool and the agent can never move funds anywhere except back to the owner's wallet.

Key features:

- - AI-driven rebalancing. The agent continuously monitors onchain rates across a curated set of audited Origin Protocol strategies (ARM Vaults, OETH, and OUSD) and reallocates automatically to maintain the strongest blended APY.

- - True self-custody. Deposits live in a smart contract only the user controls. The agent has permission to rebalance between strategies, nothing more.

- - Telegram-native experience. Users deposit, withdraw, check balances, and understand their yield in plain language through a Telegram bot. Mintly is designed to feel like a companion, not a dashboard.

- - Transparent fees. Mintly charges a 15% performance fee on yield generated, in which 50% goes straight back to the community via MLY buybacks.

### Roadmap

- Live today: ETH and USD yield products on Ethereum, three Origin Protocol strategies integrated, Telegram bot interface, self-custody smart contract architecture.

-

- TGE: MLY launches via the Virtuals launchpad on Robinhood Chain, activating the buyback flywheel with 50% of protocol fee revenue directed to weekly MLY buybacks.

-

- Post-TGE:

- - MLY staking goes live, with stakers earning a share of emissions. Depositor yield boosts roll out for MLY holders.

- - Enable deposits from Robinhood Chain.

- - Additional, more advanced Origin yield sources.

-

- Expansion: Additional yield strategies beyond the initial Origin Protocol set, broadening the sources of yield the agent can allocate across, alongside continued growth of the ecosystem emissions programme.

### Additional Details

Mintly is built on Ethereum and integrates Origin Protocol and Virtuals Protocol. The smart contract architecture uses audited, battle-tested standards, with each user deposit held in an individually owned strategy proxy. The protocol acts as a distribution layer for Origin's yield infrastructure, bringing institutional-grade on chain strategies to everyday users through a radically simplified interface. Mintly is built for the next wave of on chain users: people who want real yield and real self-custody without needing to become DeFi experts.

### Token Utility

MLY is Mintly's governance and value accrual token, with a fixed total supply of 1 billion.

- - Buyback flywheel. Mintly earns a 15% fee on all yield generated. 50% of that revenue funds weekly MLY buybacks, directly linking protocol growth to token demand.

- - Staking. MLY holders can stake to earn a share of MLY Buybacks

- - Yield boosts. Depositors holding MLY receive boosted yields on their positions, supporting growth and further protocol revenue & buybacks.

- - Emissions. Long-term growth: 240M MLY (24% of supply) is distributed over 10 years on a bell-curve schedule to stakers, depositor boosts, and ecosystem initiatives.

As TVL grows, fees grow and buybacks grow. Please checkout our tokenomics whitepaper on our website for further details. (https://mintly.so/token-whitepaper.pdf)
