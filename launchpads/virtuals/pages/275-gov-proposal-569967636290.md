### Summary

This proposal seeks to allocate 1% of total $VIRTUAL supply from the DAO treasury to establish a Sniper Defense & Yield Fund. The fund will:

- Reimburse past defense operations funded by the team

- Enable future defenses of >150 agent launches over the next 52 weeks

- Deliver enhanced yield to $VIRTUAL stakers through sniper-based airdrops

- Decentralize the decision-making of which agent launches to defend via $VIRTUAL staker votes

### Background

Since the launch of the Genesis token offering format, we’ve seen 81 agent token launches, with >75% of them reaching market caps above $600,000 shortly after launch. This creates a lucrative opportunity for snipers who enter after Virgen buyers but before the general public.

To defend against this and support fairer launches, the core team has been:

- Using $VIRTUAL owned by the core team to act as the first sniper on high-potential launches

- Airdropping the acquired tokens to $VIRTUAL stakers, proportional to their stake

This defense model has already been used to protect 20 agent launches, deploying 1,018,200 $VIRTUAL, and generating 5.3mn USD worth of tokens (at current value) distributed back to the staking community.

### Benefits

- Defends Quality Launches:
This defense fund discourages malicious actors from exploiting low-liquidity launches and preserves upside for committed contributors.

- Boosts Yield to Stakers:
Rather than random airdrops, this strategy extracts sniper alpha and channels it directly to $VIRTUAL stakers.

- Efficient Use of DAO Resources:
Deploys capital only where upside is clearest, based on real-time market and agent performance signals.

- Aligns Community Incentives:
Upcoming upgrade lets stakers vote on which agents to defend, decentralizing and aligning decision-making.

### Implementation Details

- Allocation Requested: 1% of total $VIRTUAL supply from DAO treasury

- Phase 1: Reimburse the team for prior fund usage (1mn $VIRTUAL tokens)

- Phase 2: Fund future sniper operations capped at:

- 3 teams per week

- >150 teams total over 52 weeks (~6.5mn $VIRTUAL tokens)

- A reserve pool of 2.5mn $VIRTUAL tokens for the team to utilize in case:

- More than 42,000 tokens are required for a single defense

- More than 3 teams need defense in a particularly busy week

- Voting Mechanism:

- $VIRTUAL stakers will vote to select which agent launches to defend

- Sniped tokens will be airdropped directly to the stakers who participated in the vote

### Conclusion

This proposal enhances the Genesis launch model by introducing a capital-efficient, yield-generating sniper defense fund. It retroactively compensates the team, scales the impact to more launches, and decentralizes decision-making to the community.

It deepens the utility of $VIRTUAL, makes staking more attractive, and strengthens the entire agent launch ecosystem.
