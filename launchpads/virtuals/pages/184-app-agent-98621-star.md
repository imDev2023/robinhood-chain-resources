## About

See more

In TradFi you learn who moved the market after it moved. On-chain every actor is public in real time. That transparency is crypto's edge, but only if someone reads it at scale.

Stargazer is the AI that does the reading. It breaks any token down by real behavior: organic holders, coordinated clusters, bots, and infrastructure. It shows what behavioral trend a token is in and catches the shift before price confirms it, because attention flows on-chain first.

Stargazer is building the era where markets are read as behavior 24/7, agents are born with full context, and the edge that used to belong to insiders is open to everyone. That is the philosophy Robinhood Chain was built on, and that is where we launch.

### How It Works

Stargazer processes raw blockchain data across seven networks from genesis to the latest block: Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, and Robinhood Chain, indexed from its very first block. Every address is classified by what it actually does on-chain: interaction history, timing, counterparty patterns. 200B+ transactions, no manual labels.

Three products to make on-chain behavior readable:

- - Stargazer Chat. Ask about any token and the AI reads its on-chain behavior for you. Who really holds it, what the crowd behind the chart is made of, which trend it is in. A full behavioral read in seconds.

- - Alpha Search. The free discovery layer. Scan every indexed token by behavioral filters and watch where attention is flowing across the market right now. Find what to ask before anyone is talking about it.

-

- Alpha Search Agent (coming soon).

Your own watcher on the whole market. Set flexible filters over actor clusters and behavioral signals, and the Agent monitors every transaction of every token 24/7, messaging you in Telegram or over API the moment your conditions fire.

-
