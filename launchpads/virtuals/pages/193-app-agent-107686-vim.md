## About

See more

The stock market is one of the best wealth machines ever built, and most of the world still can't reach it. Robinhood Chain changed the first half of that problem by putting real, fully backed stock tokens on-chain: Apple, NVIDIA, Tesla, ETFs, trading around the clock. Vimen exists for the second half: owning the market, not just single names, without trusting a manager.

### How It Works

Vimen is an index protocol on Robinhood Chain. A Vimen basket is a plain ERC-20 token backed by fixed quantities of real tokenized assets, held by an immutable smart contract. Buy one $MAG7 and you hold a token backed by actual Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, NVIDIA and Tesla tokens sitting in a contract that nobody can touch. Not the team, not a guardian, not anyone: there are no admin keys over funds and no oracle in the core. When you want out, you redeem the token and the exact same assets land back in your wallet. That redemption is free, permissionless and cannot be paused.

Three baskets are live today: $MAG7 (the Magnificent Seven), $AI6 (the AI giants) and $HOOD6 (the top projects born on Robinhood Chain). Behind them sits a universe of tokenized stocks, ETFs and chain natives.

### Roadmap

Phase 1: the baskets (live now)

$MAG7, $AI6 and $HOOD6 minting and redeeming on Robinhood Chain at app.vimen.org, with live NAV, one-click zap minting, and on-chain verifiable backing.

Phase 2: the Index Factory (next)

The curator system opens. Burn 5,000-25,000 $VIM for a license, then compose and publish your own basket from the asset universe: pick constituents, set weights, watch the live backtest price your recipe against real Chainlink history, publish on-chain. Curated baskets run on the same immutable contract as first-party ones, wired so curators can never touch funds or redirect fees. 60 percent of every mint fee flows to the curator's staking pool, 40 percent to the Vimen treasury, enforced by constants.

Phase 3: agents join the factory

An MCP server exposes the Index Factory to AI agents: research, compose, backtest and publish indexes autonomously, under the same license and staking rules as human curators. A thousand strategies, all fully backed, none able to rug.

Phase 4: perps on indexes

Perpetual markets on Vimen baskets, with the basket's on-chain NAV as the reference price. Long or short an entire index in one position.

Throughout

Deposit caps rising with volume and audit ($100K per basket at launch, then $500K, $2M, $10M+), more first-party baskets, deeper DEX liquidity, and integrations across wallets, DEXes and lending markets, because baskets are plain ERC-20s that anything can build on.

### Token Utility

$VIM is the token that opens the protocol up. Until now, Vimen picked the indexes. With $VIM, anyone will be able to: burn it for a curator license, publish your own basket on the same immutable rails, and earn the fees it generates.

Vimen doesn't want to be an asset manager. It wants to be the factory where the market's indexes get made: by people, and soon by AI agents. $VIM is the license to the factory floor.
