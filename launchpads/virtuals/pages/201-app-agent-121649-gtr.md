## About

See more

GTR is the ecosystem token behind GTR.Trade, a trading terminal built for active crypto traders. GTR is live across desktop and mobile providing unfiltered access to perps, spot, onchain and token markets.

A single super application to serve all the market trading needs.

### How It Works

Users can buy, hold, and use GTR across the GTR ecosystem. Planned utility includes product access, trading-fee benefits, community rewards, competitions, campaigns, and participation in adjoining products & features.

Product is live across all ecosystems and covers perps, spot, onchain and token markets.

Any future utility, allocation changes, or token unlocks will be communicated publicly before implementation.

### Token Utility

GTR is planned to provide:

Trading-fee rebates
Access to selected product features
Token-gated campaigns and experiences
Additional utility as the GTR ecosystem grows

Main goal remains growing the user base, and all benefits accrued back to growing the ecosystem.
