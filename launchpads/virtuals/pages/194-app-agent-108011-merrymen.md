### How It Works

Merrymen is a self-hosted band of autonomous trading agents for Robinhood Chain agents you never have to trust.

The one rule of the house: the model proposes, deterministic code disposes.

You mint an agent wallet locally. Merrymen generates the keys on your machine; they never leave it. There's no custody, no server-side account, no "trust us."

You set the caps. Per-trade, daily, ops/day, drawdown breaker, key expiry enforced by the account contract on-chain, not by a promise. Even a fully compromised agent cannot spend past the wall.

The agent works the market 24/7. A strategist (a built-in bot, or an LLM you plug in) proposes buys/sells; deterministic code validates every intent against the on-chain policy wall before anything is signed. Every trade is simulated first.

You start in paper mode zero funds. Fills simulate at the live on-chain Chainlink prices, so you can watch the band trade in minutes. Add a bundler key when you're ready to go live; the same wall then signs for real.

You steer it from Telegram. Chat in plain English, check the book, trade, get pings and a daily report, even control your PC all inside the same permission walls.

Anyone can ship a trading agent.

The hard, defensible thing is one you don't have to trust: your machine, your keys, caps the chain itself enforces verifiable in the block explorer.
