## About

See more

Humanoid Network is the proofed motion-data layer for Physical AI. Contributors turn natural-language tasks into humanoid motion. Hydra scores physical plausibility, quality and repeatability before accepted motions become replayable, onchain-verified training data. HAN coordinates access, contribution and validation as the network expands its motion library, robotics tools and enterprise APIs. Generated motion is abundant. Proofed motion is scarce. Any Robot. Any Task. One Network.

### How It Works

1. Create a task

Describe the action a humanoid should perform using natural language.

2. Generate the motion

Hydra converts the instruction into a complete humanoid movement sequence.

3. Validate against physics

Each candidate is tested for balance, stability, tracking quality, foot contact, and physical executability.

4. Create a verifiable proof

Accepted motions receive an on-chain proof and become replayable, auditable data.

5. Grow the network

Every validated contribution expands a reusable motion library for robotics developers, model builders, and Physical AI enterprises.

### Roadmap

Phase 1: Network Launch

Launch HAN through Virtuals on Robinhood Chain and open the public motion-generation and proof experience.

Phase 2: Proofed Motion Expansion

Expand the motion library, replay history, contributor portfolios, physics-validation coverage, and network analytics.

Phase 3: Developer and Enterprise Access

Introduce structured dataset access, developer tooling, enterprise licensing workflows, and deeper robotics integrations.

Phase 4: Protocol Participation

Activate carefully tested HAN participation mechanics, Motion Credits, access controls, and contributor incentives only as the required contracts and anti-abuse systems become production-ready.

Phase 5: Any Robot, Any Task

Extend the network across additional humanoid platforms, environments, and real-world task categories.

### Additional Details

HAN has a fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens.

Launch allocation

• 18% Liquidity Pool

• 25% Automated Capital Formation

• 25% Team Allocation

• 20% Protocol Rewards

• 7% Treasury

• 3% Early Contributors

• 2% Marketing & Advisory

Vesting discipline

• Team Allocation is locked for one year after TGE, followed by a six-month linear vest. If HAN reaches $160M FDV before the one-year mark, vesting begins immediately but still follows the six-month linear schedule.

• Protocol Rewards have a 12-month cliff followed by a 24-month linear release.

• Early Contributors and Marketing & Advisory have a 30-day cliff followed by six-month linear vesting.

• Treasury is structured with 30% immediately available and the remaining 70% subject to a six-month cliff followed by a 12-month linear release.

Reward claims and other token execution features remain disabled until their contracts, controls, and disclosures are ready.

### Token Utility

HAN coordinates access, contribution, and participation across Humanoid Network.

Core utility

• Access proofed humanoid motion-generation and robotics-data experiences.

• Support eligibility and capacity for validated motion contributions.

• Unlock future dataset, analytics, and developer-access tiers.

• Coordinate contributor incentives and ecosystem growth as verified mechanics go live.

• Support protocol operations and long-term network development.

HAN does not represent equity, guaranteed yield, APY, or guaranteed price appreciation. Staking-like mechanics, reward claims, and Motion Credits will activate only when implemented, tested, and publicly disclosed.
