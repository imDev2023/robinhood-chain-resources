## About

See more

Wizz started as a contributor network helping Web3 projects grow through bounties, content, research and distribution. Today, we're expanding that network into a data layer for AI Agents by transforming those internet signals into structured, developer-ready data.

Backed by 30,000+ users and 250+ partners, the network continuously generates real-world signals that power AI applications through DataCore.

### How It Works

Wizz has two core products: DataCore and Bounties.

DataCore aggregates on-chain, social and web data into structured data that AI agents and developers can integrate through a single platform. At the same time, the Bounties enables Web3 projects to launch bounties for content, research, education and distribution, generating valuable human intelligence alongside real-time internet data.

Together, it creates a continuously growing ecosystem where AI Agents and communities benefit from the same network.

### Roadmap

Completed ( Q1 2025 - Q2 2026)

Distributed $100K+ to thousands of contributors through completed bounty campaigns.

Q2 2027: Public DataCore API, recurring revenue reporting, staking for data providers, and community governance over data priorities and protocol fees.

### Additional Details

Buyback & Burn Mechanism

During the first 13 months after launch, the protocol will buy back and burn $WIZZ on a published schedule, beginning with a higher burn rate during the initial launch period before transitioning to a steady monthly burn.

In addition to the scheduled programme, 25% of all protocol revenue is permanently burned. Since most revenue is collected in $WIZZ, these burns are primarily executed directly from protocol revenue and scale with platform usage.

### Token Utility

$WIZZ is the native utility token used for:

Projects: to create and fund bounty campaigns. Instead of paying contributors through multiple assets, projects can launch campaigns directly in $WIZZ, creating a unified reward system across the platform.

Contributors: Every contribution, whether it's content creation, research, education, design or community participation, will be rewarded in $WIZZ.

Devs: access DataCore through API subscriptions, usage-based queries and future developer services. As more AI Agents integrate with Wizz, the token becomes the standard payment asset for accessing structured on-chain, social and web data.

Ecosystem: $WIZZ is also used to unlock premium platform features, participate in ecosystem incentive programs, receive holder benefits, and support future governance as the platform evolves.
