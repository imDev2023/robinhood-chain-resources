# LONG on Robinhood Chain Copy link

Official analytics for [app.long.xyz](https://app.long.xyz/) — every number computed from raw on-chain events, priced by the same Chainlink feeds Robinhood uses. Methodology at the bottom.

### Methodology Copy link

*   **LONG assets** = every `LaunchCreated` from LONG's TickerAirlockFactory (both deployments, `0x9c88…0845` + `0x22e9…eeED`) — verified 1:1 against Airlock creates carrying LONG's integrator address. No heuristics.
*   **Volume** = the stock-token (numeraire) side of every swap in LONG pools: v4 bonding pools (hook `0x4e34…a544` Swap events matched by poolId) plus graduated v2/v3 pools after `Airlock.Migrate`. Native-ETH pools count under WETH.
*   **Volume** counts all stock flow through LONG pools including the protocol's ~1% buyback legs (`sender = 0x6f02…0F77`); **User volume** excludes them — both are shown.
*   **USD pricing** = on-chain Chainlink feeds (hourly, forward-filled across market closes/weekends). Stock-quoted trades are never dropped for missing prices.
*   **Supply capture**: circulating supply = on-chain mints − burns per stock token (issuance is off-chain); LONG-pool holdings = cumulative pool deltas (v4, incl. buyback flows) + ERC-20 transfer accounting (graduated pools).
*   **Stock-pair share** = LONG's slice of all launchpad-token × tokenized-stock trading on the chain, any launchpad. Same pair method as the RWA trading-volume dashboard: `dex.trades` rows with a tokenized stock on exactly one side and a launchpad token on the other, Chainlink hourly fallback where Dune leaves trades unpriced; LONG legs matched against the exact launch list above.
*   **Stock-trading share** = LONG's volume ÷ ALL tokenized-stock trading on the chain: every `dex.trades` row with a stock side (spot, launchpad pairs, ETFs, commodities) plus Robinhood's Rialto venue (txs to `0x4262…c7e8`, USDG-quoted), same pricing fallbacks.
*   **Trader share** = distinct tx senders swapping in LONG pools (protocol buybacks excluded) ÷ distinct senders trading any tokenized stock on any venue.
*   **Transfer share** (table below) = every ERC-20 transfer of a stock token (mints/burns excluded); a transfer counts toward LONG when its transaction contains a LONG-pool swap.
*   Third-party dashboards undercount LONG: single-caller attribution misses one of the two factories, and `dex.trades.amount_usd > 0` silently drops stock-quoted trades Dune's price feed can't value.

Built from `robinhood.logs` / `robinhood.transactions` only. Queries are public — click any chart.
