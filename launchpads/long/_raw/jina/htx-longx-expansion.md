BlockBeats News, September 1st, according to an official announcement, Robinhood's chain issuance platform Long.xyz launched the LongX Expansion. The new feature will package Lighter's NVDA 3x leveraged position as an ERC20 token, supporting minting/redeeming, DEX pool trading, and liquidity pairing, with trading fees being distributed back to the AI token.The feature is currently in a controlled testing phase, and the team stated that they will "actively monitor the market's stability today. Depending on the results, full pairing mode may be opened up so that anyone can pair with the NVDA 3x ERC20 token."Long.xyz has recently become another hot protocol besides Pons. The meme coin Artificial Inu (AI) issued through this platform recently exceeded $160 million, and Boner, which launched 3 days ago, has swiftly reached a market cap of $70 million.BlockBeats Note: Stock Meme is an emerging trend that directly combines traditional meme coins with tokenized stocks: Meme coins are no longer paired with USDT or ETH but directly paired with on-chain stock tokens (such as NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, etc.), retaining the speculative nature and community-driven hype of meme coins while being linked to the popularity and narrative of real stocks. Transaction fees often partially flow back to the community treasury to accumulate the corresponding stock tokens, forming a dual-driven model of "emotional speculation + real asset anchoring."
