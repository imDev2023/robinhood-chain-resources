## What Is Pons?

Pons is the native launchpad of Robinhood Chain, and $PONS is its token. The platform, aka Pons Family, lets anyone launch a token on the chain, with trading fees flowing back into the protocol. Think of it as the pump.fun of the Robinhood ecosystem, but with tokenomics designed to reward holders instead of just the platform.

We flagged Pons early. It was one of the first tokens we highlighted when Robinhood Chain started trending, back in our [top Robinhood memes](https://airdropalert.com/blogs/top-robinhood-memes/) roundup. Since then, the project shipped a v2, picked up Binance Wallet support through Meme Rush, and became the benchmark every competing launchpad gets measured against.

## Token Mechanics: Burns, Treasury, and the Flywheel

Here’s where $PONS gets interesting. The token launched with a 1 billion max supply, but that number shrinks constantly thanks to aggressive burn mechanics.

The team confirmed the milestone [on X yesterday](https://x.com/ponsdotfamily/status/2087293383026905090?s=20): 27% of the total $PONS supply has now been burned. On-chain data backs this up. The burn wallet holds roughly 268 million tokens, leaving an effective supply of around 732 million.

The second engine is the Pons Treasury. It accumulates $PONS using 80% of protocol fee revenue. Every token launched on the platform generates fees, most of those fees buy $PONS, and burns keep shrinking what’s left to buy.

That’s the flywheel, and right now it’s working:

*   More launches → more protocol fees
*   More fees → more Treasury accumulation (80% of revenue)
*   Ongoing burns → shrinking supply
*   Shrinking supply + constant buy pressure → number go up

Simple, brutal, effective.

## Why the Market Cap Doesn’t Match the Price

Quick math lesson, because this confuses people. You’d expect a 1 billion supply token at 4-5 cents to sit at a $40-50 million market cap. Instead, $PONS trades at roughly a $32 million market cap at the time of writing.

The gap comes from the burns. Market cap is calculated on circulating supply, and with 27% of tokens permanently destroyed, only ~732 million count. So the price per token looks higher relative to the market cap than a standard 1 billion supply coin.

This also means comparisons to old price levels understate the recovery. The all-time high was set with more tokens in circulation than exist today. Same price, smaller supply, lower market cap — keep that in mind when you’re charting targets.

## Price Action: The Launchpad War and the Comeback

$PONS has already lived three lives in under a month:

1.   **The run-up.** The token exploded off its mid-July lows near $0.0033 and ripped to an all-time high of $0.066 on July 27.
2.   **The launchpad war.** We covered the [launchpad war](https://airdropalert.com/blogs/pons-family-vs-pools-trade/) as it unfolded. When Uniswap dropped its own launchpad on Robinhood Chain and out-launched Pons on day one, $PONS got hammered. Price collapsed all the way down to the $0.016 area as the market panicked about competition.
3.   **The recovery.** Fast forward to today, and the token trades around $0.045, up over 80% in the past week. The market seems to have concluded that burns plus Treasury buying beat panic.

A drawdown of that size followed by a V-shaped bounce tells you something. Holders who understood the tokenomics didn’t sell the war — they bought it.

## FAQ

**What is $PONS?** $PONS is the native token of Pons, the leading launchpad on Robinhood Chain. The token benefits from protocol fee revenue through Treasury accumulation and continuous supply burns.

**How much of the $PONS supply has been burned?** As of August 2026, 27% of the total supply has been burned — roughly 268 million out of the original 1 billion tokens, leaving an effective supply of around 732 million.

**Why did $PONS crash in early August?** Uniswap launched a competing launchpad on Robinhood Chain that out-launched Pons on its first day. The competition scare sent $PONS from its $0.066 all-time high down to the $0.016 range before the token recovered.

**Where can I buy $PONS?** Most volume trades on Uniswap V3 and V4 on Robinhood Chain, with additional liquidity on Up V3 and a centralized listing on MEXC.

**Is $PONS a meme coin?** Not really. While it lives in a meme-heavy ecosystem, $PONS has actual utility as a launchpad token with fee revenue, Treasury buying, and burn mechanics tied to platform usage.
