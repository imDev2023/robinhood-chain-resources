Today we’re introducing Launches (in beta), a new tab in the [Uniswap Web App](https://app.uniswap.org/launches?utm_medium=blog&utm_source=blog&utm_campaign=launches-202607&utm_content=app) where you can discover top Uniswap token launches.

Many launchpad builders like Bankr, Pons, Long, and more are choosing Uniswap as their trading infrastructure. Launches aggregates projects building on Uniswap in one place.

Start exploring top token launches on Robinhood Chain, with more coming soon.

### [](https://blog.uniswap.org/launch-aggregator-explore-top-uniswap-launchpads-in-one-place#driving-launchpad-discovery)**Driving launchpad discovery**

More than 340K new tokens launched into Uniswap across Robinhood launchpads in July alone, generating $3.6B in trading volume. Launches boosts distribution, while making discovery simple.

The tab pulls tokens from the top launchpads into a single feed. You can filter by launchpad or sort by 24-hour volume, liquidity, recently launched, or trending.

### [](https://blog.uniswap.org/launch-aggregator-explore-top-uniswap-launchpads-in-one-place#get-started)Get started

For token teams, Launches helps you get discovered alongside other projects launched on Uniswap. For traders, Launches helps you find what’s new and trade where you already are.
