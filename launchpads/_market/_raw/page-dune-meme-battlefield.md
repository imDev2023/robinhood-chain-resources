# Meme Trading Battlefield: Solana vs Base vs Robinhood Copy link

**Color legend:** Solana = purple `#7C5EEA` | Base = blue `#0052FF` | Robinhood = lime green `#CCFF00`

A large-scale comparison of meme-trading conditions across Solana, Base, and **Robinhood Chain**. Robinhood is the lime-green challenger: early, fast-moving, and measured against mature meme markets by normalized metrics, not raw volume alone.

This dashboard now tracks volume, trades, active addresses, new users, returning users, inactive/lapsed users, new meme tokens, active meme tokens, token quality, top tokens, concentration, trade-size buckets, hourly activity, wallets, and normalized opportunity scores.

## Methodology Copy link

Meme tokens are approximated as non-major assets traded on DEXs. Stablecoins, wrapped majors, and obvious blue-chip assets are excluded by symbol. Trader and active-token counts use approximate distinct counting for speed on large-chain data. Token-side metrics can double-count meme-vs-meme trades, so they are best read as market breadth and concentration proxies.
