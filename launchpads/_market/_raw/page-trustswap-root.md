04 · The full story, in plain English

A brokerage used by millions built its own blockchain. Here's the whole story — no jargon required.

**Who & what.** Robinhood Markets (Nasdaq: HOOD) — the company behind the commission-free trading app — launched its own blockchain in 2026: a fast, low-cost network built with Arbitrum's technology and anchored to Ethereum. Blocks confirm in ~100ms, fees are a fraction of Ethereum's, and markets never close.

**Why it matters.** Robinhood's bet is that stocks, crypto, and whatever comes next all end up on-chain. Tokenized stocks live in 120+ countries (not the US) — but **token launches** took off first: the chain passed Base in daily users within three weeks and crossed 100M transactions.

**The catch.** Growth attracted a documented scam wave — honeypots, copycat tickers, vanishing tokens. On a chain that minted ~18,600 tokens a day at peak, **verifiable locks are how honest launches separate themselves** — and informed users are how the ecosystem stays worth building on. That's why this hub exists.

05 · Questions, answered

## Asked constantly.

Q 1 What is MintPlus and how much does it cost?
MintPlus combines fixed-supply token creation, Uniswap liquidity setup, and liquidity locking in one guided flow. Free to start — you pay only gas, and the standard $150 lock fee is waived for MintPlus auto-locks.

Q 2 What exactly is locked — and what isn't?

The LP tokens for your Uniswap pool are locked in a non-custodial Team Finance vault with a public unlock date. Your liquidity stays yours — locked, not spent — and you earn LP fees the whole time.

[Full breakdown →](https://trustswap.com/robinhood/liquidity-locks)

Q 3 Do I need ETH on Robinhood Chain to launch?
A small amount, for gas. With one-sided pools, no upfront ETH is needed for liquidity — your initial position can be created from your token supply alone. [Bridge guide →](https://trustswap.com/robinhood/bridge)

Q 4 How do I lock liquidity on Robinhood Chain?

Connect to Team Finance, select Robinhood Chain (4663), choose your LP token, amount, and unlock date. $150 flat plus gas — or launch via MintPlus and it happens automatically.

[Step-by-step →](https://trustswap.com/robinhood/liquidity-locks)

Q 5 How much does launching cost, all-in?

Gas at L2 rates — a small fraction of Ethereum mainnet — plus the $150 lock fee if you lock manually (waived via MintPlus). Your liquidity is your own capital, not a fee.

[Full cost breakdown →](https://trustswap.com/robinhood/launch-cost)

Q 6 How do launches get featured in The Crypto App?

Locked LP, a vesting schedule, a complete token page, active socials. Editorial curation is free; promotion campaigns are open to any launched token.

[What gets featured →](https://thecrypto.app/)

Q 7 Are the contracts audited?

Yes — smart contracts are audited by CertiK, Hacken and SolidProof, non-custodial and open source.

[Read the audit reports →](https://handbook.team.finance/team-finance-security-and-audits)

Q 8 Which launchpad is best on Robinhood Chain?

Q 9 What does Launchpad review involve?
