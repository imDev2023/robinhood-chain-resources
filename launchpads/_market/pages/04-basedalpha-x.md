Today, we are launching Based Alpha, the premier launchpad for launching tokens on Robinhood Chain. Earlier, we launched a token swap feature, allowing anyone to bridge and swap assets from any chain directly into Robinhood ecosystem tokens. Based Alpha is the next stage in this release; creating a sustainable creator economy for tokens to be tradeable on Robinhood chain, all of this tradeable on one app on Based.

Based aims to be the place where you can Trade Everything and Spend Everywhere. We started offering the best-in-class experience for perpetuals trading, and we have processed more than $40 billion in volume in the last one year.. But people don’t just want to trade perpetuals. They want to trade culture, memes and RWAs. That’s why we launched Based Alpha.

About Based Alpha

Based Alpha launchpad’s bonding curve is set to be competitive and creator friendly. Tokens start on a competitive bonding curve for fair price discovery. Once successfully bonded, the token graduates and a locked liquidity pool is automatically created on the Based DEX.

Core mechanics:

Fixed Supply: All tokens are minted with a total supply of 1,000,000,000.

Bonding curve: 793.1 million tokens are available for public sale via a constant-product bonding curve for fair, automated price discovery.

Liquidity graduation: Once the curve is filled, the remaining 206.9 million tokens are automatically paired with the accumulated funds to form concentrated liquidity on the Based DEX. Liquidity is locked for long-term stability and protection for investors.

Trading: All buys/sells occur directly against the bonding curve until graduation. Post-graduation, trading continues seamlessly on the Based DEX liquidity pool

Based Alpha is creator-friendly, and 0.3% of fees from trading during bonding go directly to the creator. The remaining protocol fees goes to Based, and following the accumulation of sufficient protocol fees, will enable a fee switch with fees going back to $BASED holders.

As we scale up the launch of Based Alpha, we will be adding more QOL features over the next few days, and will be introducing an incentive program for traders on Based Alpha shortly.
