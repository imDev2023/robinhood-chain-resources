# Market context - Dealroom.co | Robinhood Chain launchpad Memecoin.fun raises $3.5M to expand cross-chain meme token platform

> Source: <https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/robinhood-chain-launchpad-memecoin-fun-raises-3-5m-to-expand-cross-chain-meme-token-platform>
> Retrieved: 2026-09-02 (Jina Reader, with links summary)

---

Robinhood Chain-based launchpad Memecoin.fun has raised $3.5 million in strategic funding. The round was completed through the USDG token and led by Becker Ventures, with participation from BitValue Capital, Mason Labs, Negentropy Capital, and angel investor Billy Wen. The company will use the proceeds to upgrade its token launchpad infrastructure, expand cross-chain bridge functionality, and develop an all-chain platform connecting meme assets across multiple blockchain ecosystems. Memecoin.fun provides token issuance tools for projects launching on Robinhood Chain. The platform aims to simplify the creation, deployment, and movement of meme tokens between different networks.
