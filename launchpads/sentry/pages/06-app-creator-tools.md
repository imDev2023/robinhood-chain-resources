The only tool shipped so far is `Robinhood bundle wallets`, and it is allowlisted per account: a fresh account is told `Creator tools are not available for this account.`
The guide's launch section says bundle-buy accounts may whitelist up to 50 wallets at launch, which is what this tool feeds.
